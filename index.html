03Someone's going on leave

Do this before their leave starts, so new enquiries don't sit with someone who isn't there

  1. 1
    Find their rulesPick their name in section 02 to see the rules they're in.
  2. 2
    Open the first rule on the listGo to Admin Settings, then Auto-assignment Rules, and click the rule name.
  3. 3
    Scroll to Select users for assigning enquiriesFind the person's name in the list.
  4. 4
    Click the × next to their nameOnly next to their name. Don't touch the territory box above it.
  5. 5
    Click SaveTop right. The change only counts once it's saved.
  6. 6
    Repeat for every rule on their listWork down the list in section 02 until you've done them all.
Enquiries they already have stay with them

Removing someone from a rule only stops new enquiries going to them. Anything already assigned to them stays on their name.

04Someone's back from leave

  2. 2
    Open the ruleAdmin Settings, then Auto-assignment Rules, then the rule name.
  3. 3
    Add them backClick Select users, teams or territories and choose their name.
  4. 4
    Save, then repeatDo every rule on their list.

05What not to touch

Leave changes are only ever about the people list

Never clear the territory box

The Assign a territory for the enquiries box must stay as it is. Removing the territory from a rule removes everyone in it at once.

ConditionsDon't change Sport Type or Enquiry stage. These decide which enquiries the rule picks up.
Rule orderDon't reorder the rules on the list page.
DeletingDon't delete or switch off any rule. If something looks wrong, flag it instead.

06Quick answers

What if removing someone would leave a rule with nobody in it?

Don't remove the last person. Flag it to Bruce or Mel first, and they'll decide who covers it.

I'm not sure if I should add someone to a rule.

Only put people into the rules section 02 shows for them. Anything else, check with Bruce or Mel first.

I made a mistake. How do I undo it?

Open the rule, add the person back or remove them again, and click Save. Section 02 always shows where they should be.

Two people are on leave at the same time.

Do one person at a time. Pick the first name in section 02, do their rules, then pick the next.
